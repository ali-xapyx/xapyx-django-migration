XAPYX Community Rules

These guidelines apply to all content and interactions on XAPYX. Violations may result in content removal, suspension, or permanent ban.

DO

Be respectful and kind to others
Share original content you created
Report violations when you see them
Build positive communities
Share constructive feedback

DON'T

Post hate speech or discriminatory content
Share explicit sexual content
Promote violence or self-harm
Harass or bully others
Share copyrighted material without permission
Impersonate others
Spread misinformation or false news

Violations may result in:

Content Removal

Posts, comments, or media that violate guidelines will be removed

Temporary Suspension

Accounts may be temporarily locked for repeated violations

Permanent Ban

Severe or repeated violations result in permanent account termination
