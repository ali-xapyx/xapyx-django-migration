Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we protect and handle your data.

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

XAPYX Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide
  • Name / Username – Your public identity on XAPYX
  • Email address – For account verification and important notifications
  • Profile picture – Your visual identity
  • Bio information – Optional information you choose to share
  • Messages and chat content – Your conversations with other users
  • Photos and videos you upload – Content you share on the platform
B. Automatically Collected Information
  • Device type – Information about the device you're using
  • IP address – For security and analytics purposes
  • Operating system – To optimize your experience
  • App version – To ensure compatibility
  • Usage analytics – How you interact with XAPYX
  • Cookies and session data – To keep you logged in and personalize content
C. Permissions (Mobile App)
  • Camera – For profile pictures and media uploads
  • Storage/Gallery access – To share photos and videos
  • Notifications – For push alerts about messages and activity

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Enable messaging and social interaction
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Send push notifications about activity
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Period.

We may share data with:

  • Hosting providers – To keep XAPYX running smoothly
  • Analytics services – To understand and improve our platform
  • Legal authorities – If required by law or to protect rights and safety

4. Data Retention

  • Account data remains active until user deletes account
  • Deleted accounts are permanently removed within 30 days
  • Backup data may be retained for up to 90 days for disaster recovery

5. Your Rights

As a XAPYX user, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Request data export
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at: support@xapyx.com

6. Children's Privacy

XAPYX is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure password hashing
  • Regular security audits
  • Two-factor authentication options
  • Automated threat detection
